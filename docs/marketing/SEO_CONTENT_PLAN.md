## Blog articles (planned)

### 1. How to start a skin photo diary (beginner guide)

- **Target:** skin photo diary app, daily skin log
- **Outline:** Why one photo/day beats memory; same angle tips; what to note (products, makeup, stress); when to see a derm
- **CTA:** Check your skin → `/scan`

### 2. Reactive skin: what to track when everything stings

- **Target:** reactive skin tracker, sensitive skin journal
- **Outline:** Lorato persona; no routine required; flare timeline; scan-before-try
- **CTA:** `/for/sensitive-skin`

### 3. Preparing for a dermatologist visit with photos

- **Target:** dermatologist prep app, skin timeline for doctor
- **Outline:** 2–4 weeks of daily photos; spot tracking; what to bring; not a diagnosis disclaimer
- **CTA:** `/for/derm-visit`

### 4. Makeup and breakouts: how to tell if cosmetics are the trigger

- **Target:** makeup caused breakout tracker
- **Outline:** Lag between application and flare; journal method; ingredient scan optional
- **CTA:** `/for/makeup-reactions`

### 5. Tracking hyperpigmentation on darker skin tones

- **Target:** hyperpigmentation tracker dark skin, melanin-rich skin journal
- **Outline:** Why generic apps fail; consistent lighting; PIH timeline expectations
- **CTA:** `/for/dark-skin`
