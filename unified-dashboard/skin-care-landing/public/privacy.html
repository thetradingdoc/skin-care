Privacy Policy

Touche · Last updated July 17, 2026 · Effective for the consumer app and web experience

Touche (“we”, “us”) is a personal skincare and medical journal. It helps you capture and organize facts about your skin, prescriptions, bills, marks, labs, and other health documents. It is a wellness / self-tracking tool — not a clinical diagnosis service and not a substitute for professional medical care.

What we collect

  • Account: email address used for one-time-password (OTP) sign-in; optional display name.
  • Captures you attach: photos and PDFs you choose to upload, plus structured fields you confirm (for example drug name, bill amount, body location).
  • Six capture tags: skin, bill, prescription, mark, lab, and other.
  • Optional AI extracts: when you use an AI read, we may temporarily store machine-extracted text from your upload to help you confirm fields.
  • Product / shelf data: products you add or scan, evaluate results, and related alerts.
  • Operational metadata: coarse device/app paths and event enums needed to run the service (not photo contents in analytics payloads).

How AI processing works

If you choose an AI read, your photo or document is sent to our capture orchestration pipeline to propose a tag and draft fields. You review and confirm before a fact is kept. Manual entry is always available and is not metered the same way as AI reads. AI suggestions (including Shelf Ask/Learn) are labeled as suggestions and are not diagnoses. We do not use your uploads to train outside (third-party) models.

How we use information

  • To provide your Calendar, Track, Progress, Shelf, and Profile surfaces from your confirmed facts.
  • To meter AI usage where applicable, and to process optional paid features (for example AI credits) through our payment provider.
  • To provide physician export / share packs that retrieve your confirmed facts — without inventing an AI narrative.
  • We do not sell your personal information. Consumer surfaces do not use third-party advertising analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics / Segment on these surfaces).

Storage, encryption, and a named limitation

Confirmed structured fact fields (and raw extract text while retained) are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM in our application layer, in addition to storage-provider encryption where applicable. Media is stored under opaque references and accessed via short-lived signed or tokenized URLs.

Limitation we want to state clearly: we do not yet operate customer-managed key rotation or a dedicated KMS workflow for these application keys. Rotating encryption keys would require a migration tool that is not shipped today. We will update this policy when that changes.

Antivirus scanning

Medical uploads through the capture pipeline are scanned with fail-closed antivirus controls (ClamAV in production configuration). Uploads that cannot be scanned safely are rejected rather than stored silently.

Retention

  • Confirmed structured facts: retained for the life of your account (until you delete the account).
  • Media files and raw OCR / extract text: purged on a schedule after confirmation windows (defaults: 90 days after confirmation for confirmed media/raw text; 30 days after creation for unconfirmed drafts). Structured facts are kept.
  • Anonymous try / funnel journeys: short-lived (on the order of days) and expire automatically.

Your controls

You can review consent language in Profile, stop using AI reads and enter facts manually, and request account deletion from Profile. Deleting your account removes related health data we hold for that account, subject to short backup/operational windows described in our operational policies.

Children

Touche is not directed to children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.
