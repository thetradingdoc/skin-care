Privacy Policy

Skin & Care · Last updated June 2026

Skin & Care helps you track daily skin photos and follow concern-based routines. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it for the consumer app and web experience.

Information we collect

Account email for sign-in, progress photos you choose to upload, routine logs, and basic device/app metadata needed to operate the service.

How we use information

To provide your timeline, routine guidance, subscription billing when enabled, and product support. We do not sell your personal information.

Photos and health-related data

Progress photos are stored to power your journal. Skin & Care is a wellness tracking tool, not a substitute for professional medical advice. You control when photos are captured and uploaded.

Face scan (before account): If you use the free skin-age scan on the web, your photo is sent securely to our analysis service to produce a wellness estimate. We do not keep that scan image on our servers after analysis; only the numeric estimate may be stored in your browser until you sign up. After you create an account, daily progress photos you choose to upload are stored in your private journal (encrypted cloud storage) so you can compare change over time.
