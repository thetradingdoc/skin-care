## Architecture one-rule

**myskinandcare is the relationship. touche labs™ is the lab that proves it. They never compete. They complete each other.**

---

## The 6 consistency rules

### Rule 1 — One tagline, two expressions

| Brand | Tagline |
|-------|---------|
| **myskinandcare** | *We don't just sell you a serum — we prove it's working.* |
| **touche labs™** | *Science-backed supplements and skincare.* |

Never swap. Never use touche labs' line on the journal; never use the journal's line on the product store.

### Rule 2 — touche labs™ always has the ™

Every instance: app footer, Shopify checkout, order email, product labels. `touche labs` without `™` looks unfinished.

### Rule 3 — The recommendation bridge must be explicit

In-app serum copy is always: **"Recommended by touche labs™"** — not "we recommend" or "try this product."

### Rule 4 — care_program ≠ product subscription

- Stripe `care_program` = journal access.
- Shopify serum = product purchase.
- Profile: *"Journal subscription · separate from product orders."*
- Shopify: no mention of `care_program`.

### Rule 5 — The proof claim lives on myskinandcare, not on Shopify

Shopify sells products (ingredients, science, clinical backing). myskinandcare proves they work. Never put "prove it's working" language on trytouche.com.

### Rule 6 — Cross-links go both ways

| Surface | Copy |
|---------|------|
| myskinandcare.com footer | `Skincare by touche labs™ · trytouche.com` *(or "Powered by…" — lock in BRAND-TP-6 / BRAND-ECO-8)* |
| trytouche.com footer | `Part of the myskinandcare ecosystem · myskinandcare.com` |
| App Profile | `Shop Skin Collection → trytouche.com/collections/skin-by-touche-labs` |

## Critical failure mode

If anyone sees touche labs™ recommending a product **without** myskinandcare journal context — standalone ad, cold Shopify visit, TikTok that only shows the supplement — the brand truth *"we prove it's working"* collapses.

**Every touche labs™ touchpoint outside the app must point back to the journal.**  
CTA always: **"Track it in the journal → myskinandcare.com."**

The product is proof. The brand is proof. Everything else is packaging.
